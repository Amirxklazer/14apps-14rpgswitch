# Îlot14: The Big 14

A top-down world game for Nintendo Switch (homebrew). Walk around the big 14 building: 8 floors up, 8 floors down,
a secret stairway next to Dalia's bedroom, talking cleaning bots, and a bot roundup minigame.

Put `Ilot14.nro` in `switch/Ilot14/` on your SD card.

Controls: left stick or D-pad to walk, A to talk or use, + to pause.

All characters, floors and dialogue are in the tables at the top of `main.cpp` (NPCS, FLOORS, PERSONS).
